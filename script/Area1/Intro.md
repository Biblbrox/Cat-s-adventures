# Sunny Days

The green of the countryside shone in the daylight, along with the scent of flowers and the gentle sound of the small river. Small and large creatures played and enjoyed the good weather.

One of them, a golden retriever, as big as he was kind and innocent, ran while smiling, letting the sun illuminate his fur so much that it seemed to be made of gold. Perhaps that's why he had that name, Aurum.

On the other hand, a dark-colored cat, as lazy as any cat, simply sat in the shade of a tree and watched his friend run.

The two were so different, but they enjoyed each other's company. And so spring and summer passed.
