# The Rumors

During autumn, a rumor began to circulate among the animals. Gato overheard one of his friends talking about troubles that had occurred in a nearby field.

Winter brings not only cold, but also beings that try to steal the light for themselves, for their darkness is so great that they try to escape it by taking the light and happiness from others.

No one knew what happened to the animals that fell victim to the dark beings, but everyone knew that they were never seen again.

A dark being had been threatening the peace of other places. Would the same happen in this field?
