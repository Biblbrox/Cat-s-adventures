Aurum: Golden retriever 
Gato/Azra: Cat

# Winter

With the arrival of the first snowfall, Gato was very comfortable, as he had the perfect excuse to stay in his little house and not move all day.

"Gato, let's go dig holes in the snow," said Aurum, wagging his tail, as happy as ever.

"Today is a perfect day to do nothing," replied Gato, stretching. "Don't dogs ever run out of energy?"

The cute golden retriever started chasing his tail while the little cat fought against his laziness.

"I'll go by myself, come with me whenever you want," said Aurum, giving Gato a big lick, who immediately hissed at him.

Gato watched the door as Aurum left; he would join him in a little while. As he went to get some milk, he thought,

"My little golden retriever, he's the most beautiful light in this place, even in winter."

Even in winter…

Gato remembered the warnings; the dark beings were near, and the brightest being in that field was his friend. The dog was so good and innocent that he would trust anyone who gave him a small caress, without seeing the bad intentions of others.

He ran out of the house, but a storm began at that moment. He couldn't see Aurum anywhere; he just tried to follow his friend's tracks, which were gradually disappearing.

"Aurum!" Gato meowed, his voice growing louder.

And so, while he searched for him, he saw a dark being near the edge of the forest take the dog and head into the heart of that sea of trees.

The cat ran with all his might, but his small legs, which were not used to running, soon failed him, and before fainting from exhaustion and cold, he only managed to hear one more bark.
